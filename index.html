About

A cabinet maker on Sundercombe Street

Freedom Cabinet Australia supplies, assembles and installs cabinetry from a workshop and showroom in Osborne Park.

Most kitchens start the same way — a room that doesn't quite work, and a rough idea of what it should be instead. We handle it from there: measuring the space, drawing the layout, making the cabinetry, and supplying the granite or engineered stone benchtop that finishes it off.

We work on kitchens, laundries and bathrooms, and that includes the parts people forget to ask about — pantries, bathroom vanities, and the run of drawers that stops a kitchen from being cluttered. Door finishes we've built recently include gloss white, semi-gloss shaker and Hampton shaker.

Because we sell direct from the factory, the pricing stays sharp, and clients regularly tell us so in their reviews. If you'd rather see and touch the finishes before deciding, the Osborne Park showroom is open six days a week and you're welcome to drop in.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does Freedom Cabinet Australia make?

We supply, assemble and install cabinetry for kitchens, laundries and bathrooms, including pantries, bathroom vanities and storage units. We also supply and install granite and engineered stone benchtops.

Do I have to pay for a quote?

No. A free measurement and quotation comes with every request. Call 0430 588 269 or send your plans through and we'll price the job.

Can I install the cabinets myself?

Yes. There are three options: flat packed for you to assemble and install, fully assembled for you to install, or fully assembled and installed by us. Several clients have assembled their own kitchens and told us how straightforward it was.

Where is your showroom and when is it open?

3/14 Sundercombe Street, Osborne Park WA 6017. We're open Monday to Friday 9:00am–4:30pm and Saturday 9:00am–1:00pm, closed Sunday. Drop in to see door finishes and stone samples in person.

Which areas of Perth do you cover?

We're based in Osborne Park and have completed projects across the Perth metropolitan area and as far south as Mandurah. If you're unsure whether we reach your suburb, get in touch and we'll confirm.

What benchtops can I choose from?

We supply granite and engineered stone. Many of our completed kitchens use a 40mm top, with options including waterfall ends and undermount or top mounted sinks. Timber tops have also featured in past projects. Talk to us about what suits your layout and budget.

What door styles do you offer?

Recent projects have used gloss white, semi-gloss shaker and Hampton shaker doors, with a range of handle and knob options. The showroom is the best place to compare finishes side by side.

How do I book a consultation?

Call 0430 588 269, email freedomcabinetau@gmail.com, or fill in the enquiry form below. We'll arrange a time to measure and talk through layout, doors and benchtop.
